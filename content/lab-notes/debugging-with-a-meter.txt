[text]
When a circuit doesn’t work, my brain wants to write a novel. The meter wants to write a receipt. The meter is usually right.

[heading] The ritual

[list]
- Is power present? (at the chip, not “somewhere on the board”)
- Is ground continuous?
- Is the signal pinned where you expect? (stuck high? stuck low?)
- Is anything getting hot?

[heading] A boring checklist that feels like meditation

[list]
- Battery voltage under load.
- Regulator output.
- Reference voltages (if ADC / op-amp).
- Continuity from connector to the pin.

[heading] The idea

[text]
Measurement collapses the wavefunction of your speculation.

[quote]
Debugging is humility with tools.

[text]
A filter is not just math. It’s a decision: keep this, ignore that. That’s basically attention.

[heading] The simplest filter

[text]
A first-order low-pass is the gentlest kind of “no”. It doesn’t reject; it softens.

[latex]
H(j\omega) = \frac{1}{1 + j\omega RC}

[text]
You can read it like this: at low frequency, the circuit is relaxed. At high frequency, it becomes skeptical.

[heading] Why I like analog filters

[list]
- They’re honest about tradeoffs.
- They don’t pretend noise can be deleted — only reduced, shaped, or moved.
- They turn “attention” into a component choice.

[heading] Practical rule of thumb

[text]
If your cutoff frequency is \(f_c\):

[latex]
f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi R C}

[text]
Pick two values that you can actually buy, then verify with a real signal.

[quote]
The world is too loud. A filter is a small act of kindness.

[divider]

[text]
If you can build one filter and explain it in plain words, you’re already dangerous (in the good way).

[text]
When I’m exhausted, I don’t want elegance. I want correctness. These three show up everywhere.

[heading] 1) Ohm

[latex]
V = I R

[text]
If you can’t estimate current, you can’t estimate heat. If you can’t estimate heat, you’re guessing.

[heading] 2) RC time constant

[latex]
\tau = R C

[text]
Not every weird behavior is “a bug”. Sometimes it’s just time.

[heading] 3) Power

[latex]
P = V I

[text]
Power is the real currency. Everything else is bookkeeping.

[quote]
Small truths. Repeated. That’s how you build things.

[divider]

[text]
These are not the only equations. They’re just the ones that still work at 2 a.m.

[text]
Sometimes I catch myself doing something like prayer — not for miracles, but for alignment.

[text]
It’s less “please change reality” and more “please change me so I stop making the same mistake.”

[heading] My non-religious prayer

[list]
- Let me notice what I’m avoiding.
- Let me be kind without needing credit.
- Let me finish what I start.
- Let me not confuse confidence with truth.

[quote]
If no one is listening, at least I’m listening.

[divider]

[text]
I don’t want certainty. I want clarity.

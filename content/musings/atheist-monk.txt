[text]
“An atheist monk” sounds like a contradiction, which is why it’s useful.

[text]
I’m agnostic about the big claims. I’m skeptical of the sales pitch. But I still want discipline, calm, and a way to live that doesn’t rot my attention.

[heading] What I keep

[list]
- Practice
- Repetition
- Silence
- Kindness (not as a virtue signal, as a survival strategy)
- The idea that you can shape your mind through habits

[heading] What I drop

[list]
- Certainty as a personality
- Guilt as a lifestyle
- The need to win arguments online

[quote]
I don’t need the universe to have a plan to act with care.

[divider]

[text]
This site is part notebook, part incense-free monastery.

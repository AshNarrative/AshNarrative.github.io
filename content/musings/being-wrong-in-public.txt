title: Being Wrong in Public
date: 2026-05-27
tags: learning, humility, writing
excerpt: I’m trying to treat mistakes as drafts, not verdicts.
---

[text]
I used to think being wrong meant being stupid. Now I think being wrong is how you learn where your mind lied to you.

[heading] My rule

[text]
If I write something and later discover I’m wrong, I don’t delete it. I annotate it.

[heading] Why

[list]
- It keeps me honest.
- It shows the path, not just the destination.
- It makes learning visible instead of shameful.

[quote]
Truth is iterative.

[divider]

[text]
Engineering taught me this: failure is data. Life is also data.

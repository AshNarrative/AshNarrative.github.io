[text]
Old websites feel like someone’s desk. Modern feeds feel like someone’s funnel.

[text]
I like rough edges. I like hand-written pages. I like links that go nowhere useful but somehow feel like truth.

[heading] What I miss

[list]
- Pages that didn’t ask me to “engage”
- Weird personal collections
- Small obsessions documented carefully
- Silence between updates

[quote]
A website can be a place, not a product.

[divider]

[text]
This site is my small room. Take your shoes off.

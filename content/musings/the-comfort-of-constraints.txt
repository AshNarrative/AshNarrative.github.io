[text]
Unlimited choice makes me anxious. Constraints calm me down.

[text]
A resistor is a constraint. So is a deadline. So is a small room. So is a simple website.

[heading] Constraints I like

[list]
- One screen at a time
- One project at a time
- A hard limit on tabs
- A small daily routine
- A budget (time or money)

[quote]
Freedom isn’t infinite options. Freedom is a system that works.

[divider]

[text]
I’m building a life that compiles.

title: The Holy of Small Things (without believing in holy)
date: 2026-05-27

excerpt: Some things feel sacred even when you refuse the word.
---

[text]
There’s a kind of reverence you can have without believing in metaphysics.

[text]
Like cleaning a desk. Like making tea. Like writing one paragraph carefully. Like fixing a bug you didn’t cause.

[heading] My version of sacred

[list]
- Attention given fully, even briefly
- Work done cleanly
- A promise kept
- A quiet room
- A tool that works exactly as intended

[quote]
Call it “holy” if you want. I call it “not wasting my life.”

[divider]

[text]
I suspect meaning is something we manufacture, like circuits: component by component.

date: 2026-05-27

excerpt: I’m not building a brand. I’m building a room I want to sit in.
---

[text]
I want my site to feel like a room with a desk lamp on. Not a billboard.

[heading] Room rules

[list]
- Easy to leave (no infinite scroll)
- Easy to wander (links are doors)
- No shouting (no autoplay, no popups, no chase)
- A few objects with stories (projects, notes, photos, recs)

[quote]
If the internet is a city, I’m building a quiet apartment.

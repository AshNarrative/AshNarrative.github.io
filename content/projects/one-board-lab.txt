[text]
I want a “lab in a slab”: a small portable board that makes experiments repeatable.

[heading] Features (target)

[list]
- Power input + protected 5V / 3.3V rails
- Test points for rails + ground
- A simple signal generator header (optional)
- A small area for prototyping
- Labels that stay readable

[heading] Why it matters

[text]
Most of my time is not spent inventing — it’s spent rebuilding the same setup and rediscovering the same mistakes.

[heading] The rule

[text]
If a setup repeats twice, it deserves a fixture.

[quote]
The monk version of engineering is: reduce friction, reduce waste, keep the essentials.

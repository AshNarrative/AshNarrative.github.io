[text]
I take scope screenshots, then lose them. The waveform becomes a ghost.

[text]
Signal Journal is a simple idea: every capture becomes a small entry with context.

[heading] Entry format (idea)

[list]
- Date
- Setup (device, probe, settings)
- What I expected
- What happened
- One guess for why
- Next test

[heading] Why this is “monk-ish”

[text]
Because it replaces frantic guessing with a calm sequence: observe → note → change one thing.

[quote]
Documentation is memory you can trust.

[divider]

[text]
If I build the tool: it will be static-first (files on disk), not a complicated app.

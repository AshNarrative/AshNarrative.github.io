I’m not trying to be a critic. These are just things that kept me company.

[heading] Music for “quiet focus”

[list]
- Ambient / drone albums when you need to stop arguing with the clock.
- Soundtracks that feel like old machines waking up.
- Anything with a steady pulse and no demand for attention.

[heading] Books (the kind that don’t shout)

[list]
- Short essays: the best form of thinking in public.
- Science history: humans touching the edge of the unknown with messy hands.
- Philosophy that admits uncertainty instead of hiding it.

[heading] Links (old web energy)

[list]
- Personal homepages with imperfect handwriting and strong opinions.
- Writeups where the author shows the process, not just the result.
- Small tools that do one job well.
